STUDENT FAQ

Q: Do I need good math grades to do carpentry?

A: No. We teach practical "tape-measure math" and fractions through hands-on cuts. Most students understand trade math in their first 2 weeks.

Q: What if I have never held a power drill before?

A: That's exactly why we exist. Our instructors guide you step-by-step from day one with complete safety supervision.

Q: Can I participate after school or on weekends?

A: Yes! We offer weekday afternoon cohorts (3:30 PM – 6:30 PM) and Saturday workshops to fit school schedules.

[ AUDIENCE 02 • SAFETY & SUPERVISION ]

PARENTS & GUARDIANS INFO HUB

We understand that entrusting your child to a building trades workshop requires complete confidence in their physical and emotional safety.

🛡️ Background Checks: Every instructor, staff member, and contractor undergoes thorough New York State child safety background screening.
🛡️ Strict Safety Standards: Eye protection, steel-toe footwear, dust filtration, and certified emergency response are strictly enforced.
🛡️ Parent Communication: Weekly attendance updates, progress reports, and invitations to open workshop viewing days.
