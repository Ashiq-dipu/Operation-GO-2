[ TRACK 01 • THE FOUNDATION ]

CONSTRUCTION & TRADES PROGRAM

Where carpentry becomes a career. Students master wood framing, power tools, safety standards, and structural craftsmanship under licensed master contractors.

▸ Curriculum: 2x4 and 2x6 timber framing, roof trusses, miter and circular saws, levels, laser measuring.
▸ Facility Scope: Delivered at our main Rochester vocational workshop, community park build sites, and through specialized security partnerships in local juvenile justice and secure youth facilities.
▸ Certification: Official OSHA-10 Construction card and Operation GO Tool Safety Certification.
Apply as Carpentry Instructor
[ TRACK 02 • PAID WORKFORCE IMMERSION ]

SUMMER YOUTH EMPLOYMENT PROGRAM

A 6–8 week paid summer intensive where youth build real community architecture and earn hourly wages, culminating in an acclaimed public showcase.

Mirrored in regional press coverage by WHAM13 and WXXI, our Summer Youth Employment program gives students real job expectations, clock-in discipline, direct deposit paychecks, and the pride of constructing community pavilions and garden gazebos across Rochester.

★ Culminating Event: City-wide public exhibition with business leaders, parents, and press celebrating student projects.
★ Financial Dignity: Youth take home over $1,200 to $1,800 in earned income to support themselves and their households.
