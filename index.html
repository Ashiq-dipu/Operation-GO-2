[ FOUNDER'S JOURNEY ]

"I GRADUATED FROM RCSD. I KNOW THE RAW TALENT IN THIS CITY."

Operation GO was born from a simple belief: when young people are handed real tools and trusted with real responsibility, their entire trajectory changes.

Justin Ortiz graduated from the Rochester City School District with a determination to build lasting economic mobility for Rochester's youth. After successfully directing high-impact youth trades programming in Charlotte, NC, Justin returned home to Rochester, channeling over 14 years of operational experience into Operation GO.

What started as a single grassroots afterschool trades program has evolved into a respected, multi-track workforce institution partnering with the ESL Federal Credit Union, the Rochester City School District, local county agencies, and commercial contractors.
